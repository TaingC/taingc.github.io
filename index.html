01 About

Hardware-to-inference, end to end.

I'm an engineer with a Master's in Electronic Systems for Embedded and Communicating Applications from Toulouse INP – ENSEEIHT, and an engineer's degree from the Institute of Technology of Cambodia. My work spans embedded firmware, FPGA signal processing, RF/radar, and computer vision — I like treating perception as a whole-system problem, from the physics of acquisition down to the algorithm.

Along the way I led a Robocon team, won first prize at a regional environmental-monitoring competition, and built a vineyard-robotics vision pipeline at LAAS-CNRS. Native in Khmer and fluent in Mandarin and English, I'm at home in international, multidisciplinary teams.
